How to play

Find words using the 7 letters in the honeycomb.

Every word must include the center letter and be at least 4 letters long.

Letters may be used more than once. Words that use all 7 letters are pangrams and earn a bonus — they appear in amber in your word list.

Tap Shuffle to rearrange the outer letters. Tap Delete to remove the last letter.
